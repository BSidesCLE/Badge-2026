1. Setup + Component picks
- The overall design was very straight forward, create a event badge with a cassette front, and designated artwork for the back -- be creative.
- Two separate badges needed to be made, one for the volunteers and one for the attendees.
- And finally, add some LEDs and make it look nice.
- LEDs, a battery, timer, and counter were selected to optimize runtime and production cost.

2. Put it all together + Creativity
- Having some playful LED illuminations was key to this project being a success. Two different display patterns were designed.
  . Volunteers - Their cassettes will 'rewind'
  . Attendees  - Their cassettes will 'play'
- The timer sends a pulse to the counter, which in turn illuminates the LEDs.
  . Attendee badges reset this counter upon startup to avoid unknown internal states. (Removes delay time if >6)
- When designing the back graphic design, some stenography was added as an Easter Egg (see more below.)
- Main electronic components apart from LEDs and power switch are located on the back of the device. They are centered within the artwork as to not disturb the imagery.

3. Technical things
- All components are SMD - no THT.
- UV silkscreen was printed on the front and back of these boards.
- Lanyard hole located at the top center of the boards.
- ChatGPT used to generate artwork (seeded with supplied concept art.)
- Kicad v.10 used to generate the schematic, pcb, and fabrication files.
- 74HC4017BQ,115 counter, 74AUP1G74GT,115 flip-flop latch, 74AUP1G14GW,125 timer.
- PCBWay was used to manufacture the PCBs, UV print the silkscreen designs on the front and back, and source and assemble the units. (https://pcbway.com/g/zR6YXM - halp plz)
